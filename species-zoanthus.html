Zoanthus

Zoanthus is a genus of colonial anemones, often called zoanthids or button polyps, that form colorful mats on reef substrates. These organisms are closely related to corals and add vibrant colors to reef ecosystems.

Coloration

Zoanthus are known for their vibrant colors, including bright greens, oranges, blues, and purples, making them popular in reef aquariums.

Growth Form

Colonies form dense mats of interconnected polyps that can cover large areas of reef substrate, creating colorful carpets.

Feeding

While they host zooxanthellae, Zoanthus also actively feed on plankton and organic particles, making them efficient nutrient processors.

Ecological Role

  • Adds vibrant colors and visual diversity to reef ecosystems.
  • Dense mats can influence substrate availability for other reef organisms.
  • Popular in reef aquariums, requiring sustainable collection practices.
