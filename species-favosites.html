Favosites

Favosites is a genus of tabulate corals, primarily known from fossil records but with some modern representatives. These corals form honeycomb-like structures with closely packed polygonal corallites.

Morphology

This extinct tabulate coral formed tightly packed hexagonal corallites with shared walls perforated by mural pores, producing a classic honeycomb cross-section.

Distribution

Favosites fossils occur worldwide in Silurian and Devonian carbonate platforms across Europe, North America, and Asia where shallow epicontinental seas once existed.

Ecological Role

As an early reef builder, Favosites stabilized Paleozoic seafloors, provided shelter for trilobites and brachiopods, and contributed carbonate mass that supported diverse benthic communities.
