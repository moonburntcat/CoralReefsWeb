Ecosystem Mosaics

Coral reefs vary across the world, from shallow fringing reefs to sprawling atolls. Each structure shapes water flow, light, and shelter in its own way.

Fringing Reefs

Located along coastlines, these reefs cushion tropical islands from waves. They thrive when mangroves and seagrass beds filter sediment and provide nursery habitats.

Barrier Reefs

Massive ridges separated from land by lagoons, barrier reefs create calm waters where seagrasses flourish and sea turtles feed.

Atolls

Ring-shaped reefs that emerge after volcanic islands subside. Their protected lagoons are nurseries for reef sharks and rays.

Energy Flow

  • Zooxanthellae algae photosynthesize and feed corals.
  • Coral polyps share energy with reef fish and invertebrates.
  • Predators keep grazers in check, preventing algae takeover.

Environmental Sweet Spot

  • Water temperatures between 23–29°C
  • Clear, low-nutrient water for sunlight penetration
  • Balanced salinity and gentle wave action
