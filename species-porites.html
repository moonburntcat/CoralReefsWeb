Porites

Porites is one of the most important reef-building coral genera, forming massive colonies that can live for centuries. These corals are known for their resilience and are among the most common corals in many reef systems.

Reef Building

Porites is among the most important reef builders, contributing significantly to carbonate production and reef framework development.

Longevity

Some Porites colonies are estimated to be over 700 years old, making them valuable archives of past climate and environmental conditions.

Resilience

Known for relatively high tolerance to environmental stressors, Porites often survives bleaching events better than many other coral genera.

Scientific Value

  • Long-lived colonies provide detailed records of past climate through skeletal analysis.
  • High abundance makes them important indicators of reef health and environmental conditions.
  • Research focuses on growth rates, environmental tolerances, and responses to climate change.
