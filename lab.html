Inside the Reef Lab

The Coral Reefs Laboratory combines field expeditions with high-tech aquaria to study bleaching resistance, reef acoustics, and restoration methods that scale.

Dr. Mei Lin · Reef Systems Lead

Oversees climate simulator experiments and mentors field teams across Southeast Asia. Dr. Lin specializes in coral-algae symbiosis and develops open-source monitoring protocols.

Prof. Daniel Rivera · Restoration Engineer

Leads the lab’s modular reef fabrication program, translating fluid dynamics research into coastal protection projects with local communities throughout the Caribbean basin.

Climate Simulators

Flow-through tanks recreate future ocean conditions, allowing scientists to test how temperature spikes and acidification affect coral calcification and symbiosis.

Genomics Suite

Portable sequencers analyze coral microbiomes in under 48 hours, helping identify probiotic strains that improve bleaching recovery.

Sound Lab

Hydrophones capture reef soundscapes; playback experiments show how restoring reef “noise” attracts juvenile fish to new habitats.

Collaborative Programs

  • Citizen science dives with open reef health data.
  • Internships pairing students with restoration teams.
  • Workshops for coastal planners on reef-friendly design.

Visit the Lab

Tours run twice weekly and include immersive AR reef exhibits, coral nursery walkthroughs, and hands-on microscope sessions.
