Residents of the Reef

More than 4,000 fish species, 800 coral species, and countless invertebrates find safety in coral structures. Meet a few of the standouts that maintain balance.

Brain Coral

Slow-growing stony corals that can live for centuries, providing unshakable habitat for small fish and anchoring reef ridges.

Parrotfish

Herbivores that graze algae and keep coral surfaces clean. Their sand-like waste literally builds tropical beaches.

Cleaner Shrimp

Set up “cleaning stations” where larger fish line up to have parasites removed—a tiny species with outsized impact.
