Safeguarding Reefs

Warming waters, acidification, and pollution have placed coral reefs in crisis. With coordinated action, reefs can recover and continue supporting people and wildlife.

Climate Action

Limiting global warming to 1.5°C preserves up to 30% of reefs. Rapid emissions cuts and renewable energy transitions are the most powerful levers.

Local Protection

Marine protected areas reduce overfishing and anchor damage, giving coral colonies space to regrow after bleaching events.

Restoration Science

Coral nurseries grow resilient fragments that can be out-planted to degraded reefs, accelerating natural recovery.

How You Can Help

  • Reduce home energy use and choose clean power options.
  • Travel with reef-friendly tour operators and gear.
  • Participate in coastal cleanups and citizen science dives.
