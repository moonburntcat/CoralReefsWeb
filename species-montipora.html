Montipora

Montipora is a diverse genus of stony corals with over 70 species, known for their encrusting, plating, and branching growth forms. These corals are important reef builders and display remarkable morphological diversity.

Morphological Diversity

Montipora species exhibit a wide range of growth forms including encrusting sheets, plate-like structures, and delicate branches.

Reef Building

As one of the most abundant coral genera, Montipora contributes significantly to reef framework development and structural complexity.

Distribution

Widely distributed across the Indo-Pacific, from shallow reef flats to deeper slopes, adapting to various environmental conditions.

Ecological Significance

  • High species diversity contributes to overall reef biodiversity and resilience.
  • Various growth forms provide different habitat types for reef-associated organisms.
  • Research focuses on species identification, growth patterns, and environmental tolerances.
