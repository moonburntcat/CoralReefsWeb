Cynarina

Cynarina is a genus of large polyp stony corals known for their solitary or small colonial forms. These corals feature a single, large polyp that can expand dramatically, creating a striking appearance on the reef.

Unique Features

The ability to expand polyps to several times their retracted size makes Cynarina one of the most visually striking corals on the reef.

Feeding Behavior

Large polyps are highly efficient at capturing plankton and organic particles, making them less dependent on zooxanthellae than many other corals.

Habitat

Typically found on reef slopes and in lagoons, often in areas with moderate water flow that brings food particles.

Conservation Status

  • Some Cynarina species are popular in the aquarium trade, requiring sustainable collection practices.
  • Their feeding strategy may provide resilience to bleaching events compared to more zooxanthellae-dependent corals.
  • Habitat protection is essential for maintaining healthy populations.
