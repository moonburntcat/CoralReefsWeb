Dive into the Coral Reefs

Coral reefs cover less than one percent of the ocean floor yet shelter a quarter of all marine life. Explore their vibrant architecture, the species they support, and how we can secure their future.

Protect reefs today →

Living Cities

Reefs grow across centuries as countless coral polyps build limestone skeletons, forming layered sanctuaries that host fish, crustaceans, and algae in delicate balance.

Coastal Shields

Barrier reefs absorb wave energy and protect coastlines from storm surges, saving an estimated $935 million in flood damages annually.

Global Lifelines

Over half a billion people depend on reef fisheries, tourism, and the genetic resources that reefs provide for medicine.

Did you know?

Coral reefs support the highest biodiversity of any ecosystem besides tropical rainforests. Their loss would ripple across food webs, economies, and cultures worldwide.
