Astreopora

Astreopora is a genus of stony corals characterized by their encrusting to massive growth forms. These corals feature distinctive star-shaped corallites and are important reef builders in many tropical regions.

Structure

The star-shaped corallite arrangement gives Astreopora its name and creates a distinctive appearance that helps with identification.

Growth Form

Colonies range from encrusting sheets to large boulder-like masses, providing structural complexity to reef habitats.

Distribution

Widely distributed across the Indo-Pacific, from shallow reef flats to deeper reef slopes and lagoons.

Ecological Importance

  • Contributes significantly to reef carbonate production and framework building.
  • Provides substrate and shelter for various reef-associated organisms.
  • Shows moderate resilience to environmental changes compared to some other coral genera.
