Acropora

Acropora is one of the most diverse and widespread coral genera, with over 150 species. These branching corals form complex three-dimensional structures that provide essential habitat for countless reef organisms.

Diversity

With species ranging from delicate branches to massive tables, Acropora dominates many Indo-Pacific reefs and plays a crucial role in reef building.

Growth Rate

Fast-growing species can extend 10-20 cm annually, making them key contributors to reef framework development and recovery.

Ecological Role

The complex branching morphology creates microhabitats that support high biodiversity, including fish, invertebrates, and algae.

Conservation Notes

  • Many Acropora species are highly sensitive to temperature stress and bleaching events.
  • Restoration programs focus on fragment propagation and genetic diversity preservation.
  • Protected areas help maintain healthy Acropora populations and reef resilience.
