Pavona

Pavona is a genus of stony corals known for their distinctive leaf-like or plate-like growth forms. These corals create unique structures that add both aesthetic beauty and functional habitat complexity to reef ecosystems.

Growth Form

The characteristic leaf-like or plate-like morphology creates vertical structures that maximize light capture and water flow.

Habitat Creation

The three-dimensional structure provides shelter and attachment sites for various reef organisms, enhancing biodiversity.

Adaptation

The plate-like form is an adaptation to optimize light exposure in various reef environments, from shallow flats to deeper slopes.

Reef Contribution

  • Unique growth forms contribute to reef structural diversity and complexity.
  • Vertical structures create microhabitats that support diverse reef communities.
  • Research focuses on growth patterns, environmental preferences, and ecological interactions.
