Acanthastrea

Acanthastrea is a genus of large polyp stony corals known for their vibrant colors and distinctive corallite structures. These corals form massive colonies with prominent, often colorful polyps.

Morphology

Characterized by large, fleshy polyps with prominent septa and often striking color combinations including reds, greens, and blues.

Habitat

Found in shallow to moderate depths, typically on reef slopes and lagoons where they can form substantial colonies.

Feeding

These corals are efficient feeders, capturing plankton and organic particles with their large polyps, supplementing their zooxanthellae nutrition.

Research Focus

  • Color variation and genetic diversity within Acanthastrea populations.
  • Resilience to environmental stressors and temperature fluctuations.
  • Reproductive strategies and larval dispersal patterns.
